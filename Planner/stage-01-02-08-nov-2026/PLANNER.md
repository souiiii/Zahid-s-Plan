A sound first pass through **Moving Charges and Magnetism, Electrochemistry and Applications of Derivatives**, with written practice, short diagnostics and chapter checks.

## Quick flow

1. Open today’s sitting below.
2. Follow its steps in order; open only the attached resources.
3. Finish the stated output and record unfinished question numbers.
4. Stop at the hard boundary. Carry work forward—never into lunch, gym, dinner or sleep.

There are **two main sittings each day**, with one protected 15-minute break inside each. The breaks do not create extra study slots.

**Weekly budget:** 35 focused hours — **Physics 11 · Chemistry 12 · Maths 12**. The sittings occupy 38½ hours including breaks. These availability limits replace the roadmap’s 42-hour estimate for this stage; no work is hidden inside meals or breaks.

## Before starting

- PDF page numbers count from the **first PDF page**, not the printed book page. In-text and end-exercise questions are different lists.
- Attempt a normal question for about **8 minutes** before checking the relevant example; allow **15 minutes** for a long derivation or optimisation problem. Close the solution and redo the question afterward.
- Video time is included in the learning step. For Neha’s video, use only the named topic; if its segment cannot be found within **2 minutes**, use the linked NCERT section immediately.
- Use the [correction protocol](PRACTICE_CHECKS.md#repair) after checking. Reading an answer does not count as solving it.

## If the plan slips

- **Started late:** Keep the hard stop. Complete the core steps in order and carry the tail forward; do not remove correction time.
- **One sitting missed:** Continue that subject’s sequence at its next sitting. Do not double the next sitting.
- **Core concept missing:** Use the named repair allowance or replace the chapter check; never test an untaught topic for a score.
- **Finished early:** Reattempt the first incorrect question, then end the sitting. No extra chapter or second long lecture is required.

Explicit repair allowances are built into Wednesday Maths, Saturday Chemistry and both Sunday sittings. They are part of the 35 hours, not additional work.
