**How to use each sitting**

- Keep one notebook per subject and one small error/carry-forward list. Open only the resources attached to that row. PDF page numbers below count from the first PDF page, not the textbook’s printed page number.
- Work in the stated order. Question numbers refer to the supplied **2026–27 NCERT PDFs**. “In-text” and “end-exercise” questions are different lists. A specified question includes all its subparts unless a subpart is named.
- The durations inside a row are budgets, not additional calendar slots. Stop at the row’s end even if an exercise is unfinished. Do not sacrifice checking to hit a question count.
- Attempt a normal question for up to about 8 minutes before consulting the relevant example; allow about 15 minutes for a long optimisation/derivation problem. After looking, close the solution and redo it. Mark it “helped”, not independently correct.
- The NCERT answer PDFs give final answers, sometimes only selected answers. Check the working against the linked section/examples as well. The included original chapter checks have stepwise marking guides.
- Video time is inside the learning allowance. Use normal playback unless a familiar passage is comfortable faster. Do not assume 2× speed. For Physics, use the exact timestamp ranges below. For Neha’s video, use only the topic named in that row; its chapter timestamps could not be verified. If locating the segment takes more than two minutes, use the linked NCERT section and named examples immediately, and keep the video for that specific doubt. No exercise depends on finding a timestamp.
- Bharat’s indexed 2022 one-shot is used for its clearly indexed explanations, **not its old exam pattern or deleted-topic advice**. The current NCERT explicitly supplies Gibbs-energy/equilibrium links, Faraday’s laws, batteries, fuel cells and corrosion where needed. A newer Bharat one-shot is linked as a backup; it is not a second compulsory lecture.

## When a sitting runs behind

1. Keep the next meal/gym/rest boundary unchanged. Record the last completed question and the exact next question.
2. At the next sitting of that subject, use its correction/repair allowance for the unresolved prerequisite before new dependent work. If that needs longer, replace the tail of that sitting’s question list. Keep the question numbers in the carry-forward list rather than silently dropping them.
3. This week has **90 minutes explicitly assigned to carry-forward repair**: Chemistry Saturday 20 minutes, Physics Sunday 30 minutes, Maths Wednesday 15 minutes and Sunday 25 minutes. These are part of the 35 hours, not extra time. In addition, each subject’s marking/correction time fixes errors from its own work.
4. If an entire sitting is missed, continue the topic sequence at the next available sitting for that subject. Do not double the next sitting. Move the chapter check if its material is still untaught, and carry the unfinished work into the next stage.
5. If early, reattempt the first wrong question in the current list, then close the sitting early. No extra chapter or second long video is required.
