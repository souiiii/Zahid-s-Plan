# Stage 1 practice checks

Original CBSE/NCERT-level practice for this planner. These are not official CBSE questions or score predictions. Attempt before opening the key. Show steps for every numerical; no calculator.

Constants: μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ T m A⁻¹; e = 1.60 × 10⁻¹⁹ C; proton mass = 1.67 × 10⁻²⁷ kg; F = 96500 C mol⁻¹. At 298 K use 0.0591 V in the base-10 Nernst equation. Small rounding differences are acceptable.

## M0 · Maths starting check · 15 minutes

1. On the integers, define aRb when a − b is even. Is R an equivalence relation? Give three brief reasons.
2. Find the principal value of sin⁻¹(−1/2).
3. Find the inverse of the matrix with rows (1, 2) and (0, 1).
4. Evaluate the determinant with rows (2, 1) and (3, 2).
5. Find k for continuity at x = 1 when f(x) = kx + 1 for x < 1 and f(x) = x + 3 for x ≥ 1.
6. Differentiate (a) eˣ sin x, (b) ln(1 + x²), (c) tan⁻¹(2x).

Mark each answer/reason group as correct, needs correction or not attempted. This is a quick screen of Chapters 1–5, not a mastery test. If two derivative parts are wrong, use the planner’s Chapter 5 repair allowance before related rates.

## C0 · Chemistry starting check · 15 minutes

1. Calculate molarity when 2.925 g NaCl, molar mass 58.5 g mol⁻¹, is made up to 500 mL solution.
2. Calculate molality for 0.20 mol solute in 500 g solvent. Explain why solution volume is not used.
3. Calculate freezing-point depression for a 0.10 mol kg⁻¹ glucose solution, Kf = 1.86 K kg mol⁻¹. State the van’t Hoff factor used.
4. Balance Zn + Ag⁺ → Zn²⁺ + Ag. Identify the species oxidised and electrons transferred per balanced reaction.

If Q1 or Q4 is wrong, repair it during the following learning allowance before concentration or cell calculations.

## P-check · Moving Charges and Magnetism · 20 marks · 45 minutes

1. **MCQ · 1 mark.** A charge enters a uniform magnetic field parallel to it. Magnetic force is (A) qvB, (B) zero, (C) qB/v, (D) dependent only on mass.
2. **Assertion–reason · 1 mark.** Assertion: a uniform magnetic field alone does not change a charged particle’s kinetic energy. Reason: magnetic force is perpendicular to its instantaneous velocity. Choose: **A** both true and reason explains assertion; **B** both true but reason does not explain; **C** assertion true/reason false; **D** assertion false/reason true.
3. **2 marks.** State Biot–Savart law in vector form and how field direction is found.
4. **3 marks.** Find the field 10 cm from a long straight wire carrying 5 A. If current is vertically upward, give the field direction at a point east of the wire.
5. **4 marks · Case.** A 100-turn plane coil of area 2.0 × 10⁻³ m² carries 0.50 A in a uniform 0.20 T field. (a) Find its magnetic moment. **(1)** (b) Find torque when its normal makes 30° with the field. **(2)** (c) State the orientation for maximum torque. **(1)**
6. **5 marks.** A galvanometer has resistance 50 Ω and full-scale current 2.0 mA. (a) Explain and calculate the added resistance to convert it into a 1.0 A ammeter. **(3)** (b) Explain and calculate the added resistance for a 5.0 V voltmeter. **(2)**
7. **4 marks.** Derive force per unit length between long parallel wires carrying I₁ and I₂ a distance d apart. State attraction/repulsion for same/opposite current directions.

## C-check · Electrochemistry · 20 marks · 45 minutes

1. **MCQ · 1 mark.** On dilution of a weak electrolyte, conductivity generally (A) increases while molar conductivity falls, (B) decreases while molar conductivity increases, (C) both increase, (D) both remain fixed.
2. **Assertion–reason · 1 mark.** Assertion: a salt bridge helps maintain electrical neutrality in the two half-cells. Reason: electrons travel from anode to cathode through the salt bridge. Use the A–D options from P-check Q2.
3. **2 marks.** Write cell notation and both electrode reactions for a standard Daniell cell. Identify anode and cathode.
4. **3 marks.** For Zn(s) + Cu²⁺(aq) → Zn²⁺(aq) + Cu(s), E°cell = 1.10 V. Find Ecell at 298 K when [Zn²⁺] = 0.10 M and [Cu²⁺] = 0.010 M. State Q and n before substituting.
5. **4 marks · Case.** A conductivity cell has cell constant 1.0 cm⁻¹ and resistance 100 Ω with 0.10 mol L⁻¹ KCl. (a) Find conductance. **(1)** (b) Find conductivity. **(1)** (c) Find molar conductivity with units. **(2)**
6. **5 marks.** (a) State Kohlrausch’s law. **(1)** (b) Express limiting molar conductivity of CH₃COOH using those of HCl, CH₃COONa and NaCl. **(1)** (c) A CuSO₄ solution is electrolysed at 1.0 A for 1930 s. Calculate deposited Cu mass; M(Cu) = 63.5 g mol⁻¹. **(3)**
7. **4 marks.** (a) Give one distinction between primary and secondary batteries, with an example of each. **(2)** (b) Write the overall hydrogen–oxygen fuel-cell reaction and explain why continuing fuel supply is needed. **(1)** (c) Write the anodic reaction during rusting of iron. **(1)**

## M-check · Applications of Derivatives · 20 marks · 45 minutes

1. **MCQ · 1 mark.** For f(x) = x³ − 3x, f is decreasing on (A) (−∞, −1), (B) (−1, 1), (C) (1, ∞), (D) all real numbers.
2. **Assertion–reason · 1 mark.** Assertion: f′(a) = 0 guarantees a local maximum or minimum at a. Reason: for f(x) = x³, f′(0) = 0 and the derivative does not change sign there. Use the A–D options from P-check Q2.
3. **2 marks.** A sphere’s radius increases at 0.20 cm s⁻¹. Find the volume’s rate of increase when r = 3 cm.
4. **3 marks.** Find intervals of increase and decrease of f(x) = x³ − 6x² + 9x + 1. Show the derivative sign chart.
5. **4 marks · Case.** A rectangular enclosure uses 40 m of fencing along three sides, with a straight wall forming the fourth. Let x be each perpendicular side and y the parallel side. (a) State the constraint and feasible domain for x. **(1)** (b) Express area in x. **(1)** (c) Find dimensions and maximum area, justifying the maximum. **(2)**
6. **5 marks.** Find the absolute maximum and minimum of f(x) = x³ − 3x on [−2, 2]. Show critical points, endpoint values and all locations attaining each extremum.
7. **4 marks.** Two positive numbers sum to 20. Find the numbers with maximum product and justify the maximum using derivatives and the domain.

## Correction protocol

For each lost mark:

1. Write the corrected step and one cause: **concept, formula, algebra/arithmetic, units, wording or incomplete justification**.
2. Close the key and redo the failed question.
3. Add its question number to the carry-forward list if it still needs help.

A corrected answer is not an independent first-attempt mark. **16/20 with no whole topic blank** is a useful progress signal, not an official CBSE standard. Below that, use the named repair allowance and carry unresolved topics forward before harder dependent work. Even a high score does not mean every NCERT question is complete.
