The post–Phase 1 windows are **provisional coverage targets**, shortened slightly to accommodate the restart. They are not a promise that every chapter will fit at an untested speed. Check actual progress on 4 November before building the next phase. If it slips, redistribute learning time explicitly; do not quietly remove examinable topics or postpone all revision. There is no dedicated late-December revision-only week.

**Phase 1 day design — three subject slots plus revision, 5 hours 45 minutes scheduled:**

| Time | Study block |
|---|---|
| 11:30 AM–1:15 PM | Maths, 105 minutes |
| 1:15–3 PM | Physics or Solutions on alternate days, 105 minutes |
| 9:30–11 PM | Organic foundation, 90 minutes |
| 11:15 PM–midnight | Continuous revision, 45 minutes |

Long subject blocks include a five-minute pause within their allotted time. The daily total includes revision and these short pauses; it is not six hours of uninterrupted concentration. No study before 11:30 AM, during 3–9:30 PM, or after midnight. Lunch/gym 3–8 PM and dinner 8–9:30 PM remain protected; they do not appear as highlighted blocks or calendar events. The 11–11:15 PM gap is also left clear. No compulsory late buffer is added.

Maths and organic appear daily. Physics runs on **26, 28, 30 Oct, 1 and 3 Nov**; Solutions runs on **27, 29, 31 Oct, 2 and 4 Nov**. This gives longer learning/practice sessions without adding a fourth subject slot. Later phases should retain the three-slot limit and move time toward demonstrated needs.

**Essential organic base:** structures and functional groups; basic naming/isomers; bond fission and intermediates; nucleophiles/electrophiles and electron arrows; inductive/resonance effects; limited stability/acidity/basicity comparisons; reaction families. The revised phase gives these smaller steps more practice time. No full Hydrocarbons course, purification/analysis theory or advanced mechanism catalogue is added. Move into Haloalkanes when naming, electron movement and simple reactivity are comfortable; repair precise gaps before proceeding.

**Marks-focused filter:** current CBSE theory scope → NCERT concepts/examples/exercises → selected current-syllabus board questions. Prioritise standard numericals, required derivations, diagrams, explanations, reaction conditions, conversions and tests. Repeat errors rather than collecting equivalent questions. Lecture completion is not chapter completion. Low historical frequency does not prove an in-syllabus topic cannot be asked; retain all examinable chapters. Practical/internal requirements remain separate.

**Continuous revision:** 45 minutes within the daily total, starting with already-covered chapters and then returning to material actually taught approximately 1, 3 and 7 days earlier. Later phases add 14- and 30-day returns. Recall first, check the maintained note second, and save only useful corrections. Revision references must follow the revised teaching dates, not the discarded schedule.

**AI-maintained notes:** [chapter cards](Notes/REVISION_NOTES.md) hold concise concepts and personal corrections when reported. The website links cards from the checking step. Browser progress/corrections are exported for AI updates; they do not automatically rewrite the notes or sync with calendar completion. If work overruns, record the exact item and use the start of the next same-subject block for repair, replacing explanation/replay time first. Larger backlogs need replanning.
